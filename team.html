Lineage & Vision

Sachin Subarao Patli

Founding Custodians

For decades, Hari Om Silver House has eschewed modern mass production in favor of dedicated silver craft. Every curve, engraving, and polish is overseen by traditional artisans who treat silver not merely as metal, but as a living canvas of family heritage.

"True luxury lies in patience. A silver piece shouldn't just sparkle today; it must carry its weight, purity, and story across generations."

Operating from our historic ateliers in Pune and Vita, our atelier bridges ancient Maharashtrian silverware artistry with timeless modern aesthetics.
